# Africa Opportunity Map index

## Fifty-four markets. One operating question each week.

The Africa Opportunity Map is a year-long study of where productive opportunity sits across the continent. Each entry separates the market read from the practical application: sector sizing, entry routes, risks and positioning.

### Published

**01 / Nigeria** — The opportunity is in productive bottlenecks  
Energy systems, industrial capacity, logistics and the operating infrastructure around a large, reforming economy.

### The sequence

One country per week for fifty-two weeks, with two smaller countries covered in each of the final two weeks. The calendar may change when events materially alter the order in which markets should be read; the commitment to all fifty-four countries does not.

# About

## The research arm of BuildX.

BuildX Research studies the markets, policies and productive systems that determine where private capital can create durable value across Africa.

It is produced in the firm voice of BuildX. Named analysts will be credited as the team grows, while the institution remains accountable for the work.

BuildX Research is independent of ZeroToAct. ZeroToAct's weekly Signal may reference published BuildX Research with clear attribution. BuildX Research does not promote the ZeroToAct community.

The Annual Outlook is a BuildX Research product. It may be presented at the ZeroToAct Summit, but its research ownership, editorial control and identity remain with BuildX.
