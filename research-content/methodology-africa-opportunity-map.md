# Africa Opportunity Map methodology

The Africa Opportunity Map is designed to answer a practical question: **where can productive effort and capital create durable value now?**

## Country selection

The programme covers all fifty-four African countries over one year. The order is editorial rather than a ranking. We prioritise markets where a policy shift, infrastructure build-out, price change, regulatory transition or operating inflection makes the timing especially useful. Two smaller countries are paired in each of the final two weeks so that every market receives a considered entry.

## Evidence

We begin with primary material: national statistics, legislation, regulatory decisions, budgets, central-bank releases and company filings. We then use multilateral datasets and reputable industry sources to test scale, direction and comparability. Sources are linked or named in each report. Where evidence conflicts, we state the conflict rather than smoothing it away.

## Analysis

Each country is assessed through five lenses:

1. **Demand:** who needs the product, service or infrastructure, and what can they pay?
2. **Productive constraints:** which bottlenecks are expensive enough to support a real market?
3. **Market structure:** who controls access, distribution, licences, data and customer relationships?
4. **Capital fit:** what kind of capital, operating capability and time horizon does the opportunity require?
5. **Execution risk:** currency, policy, security, infrastructure, counterparties and exit.

## Conclusions

We separate the report into two halves. The first gives a complete market read: what the country is, what changed and why it matters. The second turns that read into application: sector detail, sizing logic, entry routes, risk and positioning for career builders, operators and investors.

We do not treat macroeconomic growth as proof of opportunity. An opportunity must have a defined customer, a solvable constraint, an executable route to market and a return profile appropriate to the risk.
